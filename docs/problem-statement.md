# Problem Statement

## Who is affected?
Users who access and share Quranic content through digital platforms may encounter Quranic verses in the form of screenshots, scanned pages, or digital images, making the text difficult to verify directly.

## What exactly is going wrong?
The problem is that text contained in an image is not immediately available as machine-readable text, while Arabic Quranic text also contains character forms and diacritics that can make automated text recognition challenging.

## Why does it matter?
This matters because inaccurate or modified Quranic text shared online may be difficult for users to identify without manually comparing it with a verified Quranic reference.
